\vspace{1em}

This appendix contains the complete, unedited output of the statistical
software for all 30 denominator-gradient models reported in the manuscript and
its supplement: a pooled fit and a within-starter fit for each of the eight
men's and seven women's leagues. Models appear in the order of the manuscript's
tables, men's leagues first.

Each model is fitted by ordinary least squares of log recorded minutes on
prior-seven-day exposure per 90, with weekly and half-weekly calendar-phase
terms and a covariance clustered on player identifier. The coefficient on
exposure is the denominator gradient $\gamma$.

The output is generated by \texttt{src/42\_statistical\_output\_appendix.py},
which refits every model from the deposited appearance data and compares the
result against the gradient already published in the corresponding league
table. A disagreement beyond floating-point tolerance stops the run rather than
printing. This appendix is therefore the output of the models that produced the
published numbers, and cannot quietly become the output of any other model.
